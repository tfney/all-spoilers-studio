
## Part 1: Core Identity & Purpose

- **The Brand:** **All Spoilers** (The polished public-facing blog) & **All Spoilers Studio** (The public digital garden and open-source workshop). Visitors can read the final cuts on the blog or click over to the Studio to watch how those thoughts were built.
    
      
    
- **The Persona:** An observant, sharp-witted person sitting on the couch, analyzing why a story works—or completely falls apart. Not an industry insider or an academic film professor.
    
      
    
- **The Philosophy:** Most media sites hide behind spoiler warnings and rush to publish hot takes the week a show drops. All Spoilers operates **post-hype and full-spoiler**. You cannot properly dissect character arcs, structural flaws, or themes while walking on eggshells around the plot. **No plot points spared.**
    
      
    
- **The Tone:** Conversational, grounded, unpretentious, and direct. It reads like a late-night thought spiral shared with a friend.
    
      
    

## Part 2: The Two-Site Architecture

1. **The Main Blog (All Spoilers):** Where polished essays, quick observations, and vibe checks live.
    
      
    
2. **The Digital Garden (All Spoilers Studio):** Linked directly from the main blog, showing your raw notes, half-baked theories, and evolving connections in real-time.
    
      
    

## Part 3: The Content Formats

- **Deep Dives:** Conversational essays exploring single concepts or theme comparisons across multiple shows.
    
      
    
- **Quick Scans:** Fast observations, single-scene breakdowns, or micro-notes on weird narrative quirks.
    
      
    
- **Vibe Checks:** Casual recommendations and post-hype verdicts based on moods rather than sterile star ratings.
    
      
    
- **The Dabble:** Low-pressure creative writing sketches or worldbuilding vignettes.
    
      
    

## Part 4: The Local Vault & Workflow (Obsidian)

To prevent administrative overanalysis and keep internal links unbroken, your vault uses a **flat folder structure** with zero moving parts:

  

Plaintext

```
all spoilers studio/
├── 00-Inbox/      <-- The temporary parking lot for raw thoughts (48-hour rule)
└── Notes/         <-- Everything else lives here forever (preserving internal links)
```

### The Status Lifecycle (Obsidian Properties)

At the top of your markdown notes, you track where a piece is in the pipeline using Obsidian's visual Property boxes:

  

- `status: couch-notes` $\rightarrow$ Raw, chaotic thoughts captured immediately after watching an episode. (Format property remains blank here).
    
      
    
- `status: writers-room` $\rightarrow$ Active notes where ideas are clustered and built into an argument.
    
      
    
- `status: on-air` $\rightarrow$ The finished, polished essay spun out into its own separate note and published live to the public blog.
    
      
    

### The Metadata System: Keeping Order Without Fatigue

To avoid blowing up your tag list with a hundred micro-genres, metadata is split into two clean layers:

  

1. **The Macro-Genre Property (The Literal Container):**
    
      
    - _Rule:_ Keep it broad to avoid tag bloat. Use a single property like `genre:` restricted to a tight list (e.g., `crime-drama`, `comedy`, `prestige-drama`, `reality-tv`).
        
          
        
2. **Plain-English Phenomenon Tags (The Human Behavior):**
    
      
    - _Rule:_ Reserve tags strictly for recurring human behaviors, psychological traps, and social patterns you spot across multiple shows.
        
          
        
    - **Core Vibe / Behavior Tag Options:**
        
          
        - `#loneliness` (Isolation surrounded by people or manicured spaces)
            
              
            
        - `#stuck` (The feeling of being trapped in a life or routine you hate)
            
              
            
        - `#pretending` (Performing happiness or normalcy for an audience)
            
              
            
        - `#acting-out` (Self-sabotage and sudden explosive rebellion)
            
              
            
        - `#hidden-anger` (Resentment simmering just beneath polite small talk)
            
              
            
        - `#suburbia` (The specific claustrophobia of manicured neighborhoods)
